Privacy Policy - MN-Doc App

Last updated: August 2026

Quick summary: MN-Doc runs entirely on your device. Your files, documents, and photos are never sent to any server we own — we don't operate a server at all. The only exception is if you personally choose to enable the optional AI features (which require your own free Google Gemini API key).

1. Information the app collects

MN-Doc does not collect or store any personal data on external servers. All of the following data stays on your device only:

  • Files you open or create (PDF, images, text)
  • The name you optionally enter in your local profile
  • App settings (language, theme)
  • Saved signatures and stamps
  • Your Gemini API key, if you choose to enter one

2. Permissions the app requests and why

  • Camera: to scan documents, recognize text (OCR), and read QR/barcodes.
  • Microphone: for the voice dictation feature (speech-to-text).
  • Storage/Files: to open and save your files (PDF, images, text) on your device.
  • Internet: used only when you optionally use AI features (smart summarization, chat) or when checking for app updates.

3. Third-party services

The app relies on the following services:

  • Google ML Kit (text recognition, translation, barcode scanning, document edge detection): runs entirely on your device (on-device processing) and does not send any data to Google's servers.
  • Google Gemini API (optional, for smart summarization, AI chat, and AI-powered text recognition): if you choose to enter your own API key and use these features, the text or image you process is sent to Google's servers for processing. See Google's Privacy Policy. This usage is entirely optional and requires no involvement from us, since we do not operate any server.
  • GitHub (update checking only): the app occasionally checks for new versions via the public GitHub API, without sending any personal data.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or rent any of your data to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes. The app contains no ads and no analytics/tracking tools.

5. Data security

All your files and settings are stored locally on your device using Android's standard secure storage mechanisms. We cannot access them, since we do not operate a server that receives them.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it entirely at any time by uninstalling the app, or through the deletion options available within the app itself (such as deleting files or clearing settings).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any update will be posted on this page along with the updated date above.

9. Contact us

For any questions regarding this policy, you can reach out by opening an "Issue" on the project repository: github.com/awsnuaimi/mn.doc/issues
